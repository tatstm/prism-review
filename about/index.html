About PRISM

PRISM: A Youth Journal of History and Social Science publishes original research by secondary-school students in history and the social sciences, broadly understood. It is edited by students at Milton Academy and accepts papers from students at any school; Volume VI, Issue I includes work from Milton Academy and Shanghai High School International Division.

PRISM's first issue, Volume I, Issue I, appeared in Fall 2021. Beyond publishing, PRISM supports young researchers through seminars, speakers and help with original research.

Every submission is screened by the editors, read by a reviewer, and decided by the Editorial Board; the submission guidelines give the details.
